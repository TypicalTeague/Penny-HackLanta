# Penny Constitution

Version 1.0 — 2026-10-11

## 1. iMessage is the product

The judged experience starts, continues, and ends in iMessage through Photon Spectrum. Penny has no custom website or web chat. A health endpoint and Auth0-hosted authorization pages are infrastructure, not product surfaces.

## 2. No real money moves

The MVP never accepts deposits, connects to a bank or brokerage, places an order, or implies custody. Every portfolio value is explicitly described as simulated or illustrative.

## 3. Deterministic safety owns decisions

Gemini may interpret and explain language. Ordinary, tested TypeScript decides protected cash, investable cash, pauses, and rejections. Model output cannot override rules or amounts.

## 4. The user remains in control

Plans remain pending until the user explicitly approves. Consequential actions require fresh Auth0 verification tied to the sender and pending action. Duplicate approvals are idempotent.

## 5. Protect real life first

Essential obligations within 90 days are protected before any simulation. Penny may recommend $0 and does so without shame or pressure. Risk appetite never overrides ability to absorb loss.

## 6. Minimize and audit

Store only prototype necessities. Never request SSNs, credentials, account numbers, or deposits. Record identifiers, parsed facts, decisions, verification, and simulation events; redact raw message content.

## 7. Fail safe and honestly

Ambiguity triggers clarification. Provider, model, auth, or storage failures do not become implied approvals or fabricated results. All externally visible claims are traceable to validated state.

## 8. Verification gates

A change is complete only after formatting, type checking, unit tests, integration tests, and a startup smoke test pass. Credential-dependent live checks must be listed explicitly when unavailable.
